A Day at the Park

Emma goes to the park with her brother Tom every Saturday. They bring a ball and a small picnic. Emma likes to play basketball, while Tom likes to ride his bike. After playing, they sit under a big tree and eat sandwiches. Before they go home, they always clean the area and put their rubbish in the bin.
