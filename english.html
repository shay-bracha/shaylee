The Secret Map at School

On Monday morning, Maya and Daniel were cleaning an old classroom when Maya found a small, dusty box under a table. Inside the box was a colorful map with a big red star on it.

“Look! I wonder what this is,” Maya said.

The two friends showed the map to their teacher. The map had three clues: a picture of a book, a number seven, and a drawing of a tree. Maya and Daniel were excited. They decided to follow the clues during their lunch break.

First, they went to the library and found a book with a small note inside. The note said, “Look where the biggest tree grows.” They ran outside and found the biggest tree in the school garden. Under a bench nearby, they discovered a tiny box.

Inside was a message: “You solved the mystery! The real treasure is curiosity. Keep asking questions, exploring, and learning!” Maya and Daniel smiled. They had not found gold, but they had found something even better: a new adventure.

Questions

1. Where did Maya find the dusty box?

2. What was on the map?

3. Where did Maya and Daniel find the first note?

4. What did they find under the bench?

5. What was the real treasure?
