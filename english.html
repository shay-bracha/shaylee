Questions Children Ask

Children are curious and love to ask questions. Mia asks, “Why is the sky blue?” Tom asks, “How do birds fly?” Sarah wants to know, “Where does rain come from?” Their teacher smiles and tells them that asking questions is a great way to learn. The children listen, think, and ask even more questions every day.
